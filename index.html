About Me

Professor of Electrical & Computer Science Engineering and Dean (Academic Affairs)

With a distinguished academic career spanning over 28+ years, I am committed to advancing the field of robust control systems through rigorous research and innovative teaching methodologies. My research work is focused on Robust Controller design, Autonomous systems, Cyber-physical systems, Multi-Agent Systems, Robotics, and AI-enabled Control Systems.

I hold a PhD degree from Indian Institute of Technology Bombay in 2009 and since then contributed significantly to research with 90+ publications including 5 monographs, 3 proceedings with over 1000+ citations from 55+ countries. My research work is useful in several civilian applications like Smart/Micro grid, Traffic and Collision Avoidance System, Coordination of Robotic Teams, Drone Swarm and Formation and many more.

In my administrative roles as Dean of Academic Affairs and Asso. Dean of Research & Development, I have led curriculum reform, student enrolment, accreditation and ranking processes, inter-department coordination, research policy development, and industry–academia collaboration, convocation. My roles also involved managing academic programs, offer proactive and data-driven guidance on academic matters to the leadership, new program offerings, student success, and faculty evaluation.

Research & Projects

My research focuses on advancing control theory and its applications in modern engineering systems, with emphasis on robust and intelligent control methodologies.

Research Areas

  • Sliding Mode Control (Discrete-time, Networked control, Higher order, Event triggered).
  • Control of Multi-Agent & Distributed Systems (Consensus, Homogeneous, Heterogeneous Systems).
  • Autonomous Systems (Robotic manipulator, Quadcopter swarm, Smart grid, Power converters).
  • Cyber-Physical System, AI-Enabled and Learning-Based Control.

Recent Research Contributions

  • Robust sliding mode protocols for quadcopter swarm formation and tracking
  • Discrete-time sliding mode consensus protocols for leader–follower multi-agent systems
  • Networked sliding mode control addressing network irregularities
  • Sliding mode controllers for power electronic converters used in EVs and solar systems
